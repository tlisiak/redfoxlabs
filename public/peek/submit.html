Submit a view

Submissions open soon. In the meantime, take a peek at what's already here.

Know a live camera that deserves a spot on Peek? Tell us about the place and we'll review it. Approved views appear exactly like the rest, with the same info card.

What we're looking for
  • A 24/7 live YouTube stream that allows embedding (a recording, loop or replay won't work).
  • A fixed or slowly panning view of somewhere beautiful, in HD, run by you, your venue, a park, a town, a tourism board or another trusted operator.
  • Not music-over-scenery loops, multi-camera rotations, news, indoor or street-corner cams.
  • You'll write the info card (what you're looking at, what to watch for, the story, sources). Plain and accurate beats flowery, and we edit for typos before publishing.
  • You must run the stream or have the operator's blessing to share it.
